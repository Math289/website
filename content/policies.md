## Eligibility & enrollment

This course is for current math PhD students only. It is not open to undergraduates or master's
students.

Permission from the instructor is required to enroll. If you're interested, please contact Otis,
Shurui, Fred, or Henry.

## Grading

1. **Attendance is mandatory.**
2. **Final project:** a midterm proposal, followed by a final demo/presentation.

## Collaboration policy

Attendees are encouraged to discuss and collaborate together.

This is a course about using AI tools for mathematical research, so we expect you to use
generative AI — treat generative AI tools as collaborators. However, outsourcing everything to
generative AI without actual engagement is a violation of the Honor Code.

## Honor code

All students are expected to uphold the [Stanford Honor Code](https://communitystandards.stanford.edu/policies-guidance/honor-code)
in all coursework.
